About the Toyota Fortuner

The Toyota Fortuner is a popular suv in the used car market. Features and condition differ from car to car, so confirm what this exact car has with the dealer.

Check before you buy

  • Match the RC, insurance papers and service records with the car and its chassis number.
  • Compare the odometer with the service history and ask for all keys and the owner’s manual.
  • Look for uneven panel gaps or paint mismatch that can point to accident repairs.
  • Test drive on city roads and a highway stretch; check the gearbox, brakes, suspension and AC.
  • Ask for the full on-road price including transfer charges, and show your InventoryDekho coupon code before you close the deal.
