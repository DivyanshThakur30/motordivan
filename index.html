Used car FAQs

How do I find used cars in my city?

Select your city on this page. MotorDivan then opens that city's page, where you can filter by brand, price, KM driven, year, fuel, transmission and colour.

Why do I have to choose a city first?

Used cars are sold by local dealers, so listings are organised city by city. Choosing your city shows only cars you can actually see and test drive.

Are the dealers verified?

Dealers listed on MotorDivan are checked before their cars go live, and every listing shows the dealer's name and location on a map.

Does MotorDivan sell the cars?

No. MotorDivan is a listing aggregator. Cars are owned and sold by independent dealers, and MotorDivan connects you with them.

Can I get a loan or insurance for a used car?

Yes. Loan and insurance offers from MotorDivan partners are available for used cars. See the loan and insurance links on this page.

Is the listed price final?

The price is set by the dealer. Final price, paperwork and any offers are confirmed with the dealer.

Why is it better to see all dealers' cars in one place?

You save time and can compare easily. Instead of visiting many showrooms or calling each dealer, you see cars from many verified dealers together, filter by brand, budget, KM driven, year and fuel, compare price side by side, and contact only the dealers whose cars you like.

How do I get the MotorDivan discount coupon?

Fill in the enquiry form on this page and you will get a coupon code right after you submit. Show that coupon code to the car dealer at the time of buying the car to get a discount. You also get a MotorDivan Premium Welcome Kit when you buy a car through MotorDivan.

How does MotorDivan help used car dealers?

Dealers can list their showroom and show their whole stock to buyers in their city. MotorDivan sends interested buyers to the dealer, while the dealer keeps full control of the cars, the price and the sale. Dealers can also have their cars shared on YouTube and Instagram. Visit the For Dealers page to list your dealership.
