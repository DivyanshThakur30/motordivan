Used car FAQs

How do I find used cars in my city?

Select your city on this page. MotorDivan then opens that city's page, where you can filter by brand, price, KM driven, year, fuel, transmission and colour.

Why do I have to choose a city first?

Used cars are sold by local dealers, so listings are organised city by city. Choosing your city shows only cars you can actually see and test drive.

Are the dealers verified?

Dealers listed on MotorDivan are checked before their cars go live, and every listing shows the dealer's name and location on a map.

Does MotorDivan sell the cars?

No. MotorDivan is a listing aggregator. Cars are owned and sold by independent dealers, and MotorDivan connects you with them.

Can I get a loan or insurance for a used car?

Yes. Loan and insurance offers from MotorDivan partners are available for used cars. See the loan and insurance links on this page.

Is the listed price final?

The price is set by the dealer. Final price, paperwork and any offers are confirmed with the dealer.
