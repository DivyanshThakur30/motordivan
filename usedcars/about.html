ℹ️ Who we are

About MotorDivan

Buying a car is one of the biggest decisions many families make, and it should not feel confusing. MotorDivan was built to make it simpler: clear information about cars, honest comparisons, and an easy way to find trusted dealers.

We bring together car research and a used car marketplace in one place. You can learn about a model, compare variants and prices, then browse real cars listed by verified dealers and contact the dealer directly.

Our used car marketplace starts in Bhopal, with more cities on the way.

One thing to be clear about

  • ✔ We don't own any car
  • ✔ We connect customers with dealers
  • ✔ Dealers set the price and make the sale
  • ✔ You always deal directly with the dealer

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MotorDivan?

MotorDivan is an automotive website where you can research cars, compare options and browse used cars from verified dealers. We connect customers with dealers.

Does MotorDivan sell cars?

No. We do not own, buy or sell any car. Every car is owned and sold by an independent dealer, and MotorDivan is not a party to the sale.

What does the Verified Dealer badge mean?

Dealers that have been verified by MotorDivan carry a Verified Dealer badge on their page and on their cars.

Where can I watch MotorDivan car videos?

We put videos of cars on YouTube (@motordivan) and Instagram (@motordivan).

How can I list my dealership?

Visit the For Dealers page and fill in the listing form. The MotorDivan team will get in touch with you.

Please note: MotorDivan is a listing platform. All vehicles are owned and sold by independent third-party dealers, and MotorDivan is not a party to the sale. Have a question? Contact us.
